\chapter*{UN Sustainable Development Goals} \label{chap:UnitedNations}

In our fast-changing world, the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) set out a shared plan for a fairer and more sustainable future. Adopted by the United Nations in 2015, the 17 goals address global challenges such as poverty, inequality, climate change, environmental degradation, peace, and justice, with a target horizon of 2030. Reaching them depends, in good part, on the innovative technological solutions that drive progress.

This page examines how the present work, a communication system that keeps autonomous mobile robots connected by relaying data through one another even where no network infrastructure exists, aligns with the SDGs. The analysis identifies the goals and targets that the work most directly supports, and the indicators by which that contribution could be measured (Table~\ref{tab:sdg}).
