\chapter*{UN Sustainable Development Goals} \label{chap:UnitedNations}

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), adopted by the United Nations in 2015, are a set of 17 goals that frame a shared agenda for a fairer and more sustainable future by 2030, spanning challenges as diverse as poverty, inequality, climate change, and justice. Technology is one of the levers through which that agenda advances.

This work, a communication system that keeps autonomous mobile robots connected by relaying data through one another even where no network infrastructure exists, contributes to that agenda in concrete ways. Table~\ref{tab:sdg} maps the goals and targets it most directly supports to the indicators by which each contribution could be measured.
